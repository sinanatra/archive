Ever Again?

In connection with the recent EVER GIVEN incident in the Suez canal, we asked ourselves whether this could potentially ever happen again.
Focusing on a selection of ships of the Evergreen shipping company and their respective routes that pass through the Suez Canal, a visualisation highlights the next possible date on which the respective ships will pass through the canal and could thus hypothetically get stuck again.

The information shown was collected by students of the Rhine-Waal University of Applied Sciences on 19 April 2021.
